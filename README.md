# Mythwood Forest

A first-person PSX-style horror game developed solo in Godot 4.

You play as a monster hunter who receives an anonymous tip about a myth 
sighting in Mythwood Forest. Your car is ambushed on the way, you survive, 
crash near the forest entrance, and head in looking for help. What you find 
instead is an empty campsite, you listen to the recording left behind and a
chill runs down your spine, where did the entrance go?

Uncover the lore. Find the cabin. Use the holy bullet. Don't miss.

> Currently in development.

## Gameplay Features

- First-person exploration with sprint, inventory, and item interaction
- Tape recorder collectibles that reveal the story across multiple locations
- Enemy AI with idle and chase states
- Functioning weapons to defend yourself
- multiple endings based on player choices
- PSX aesthetic — low-poly models, retro rendering, limited color palette
- Original 3D environments including a campsite, graveyard, and forest

## Built With

- Godot 4 (GDScript)
- Blender — character rigging, custom animations, 3D modeling
- Optimized grass rendering via MultiMeshInstance3D
